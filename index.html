A b o u t M e

I am the Head of Computer-Using Agents (CUA) at Beipo (北坡), where I lead NorthCUA from zero to one: an experience-driven loop that connects a unified agent harness, experience data, model training and continual learning for agents that operate real desktop software and the web. Its open Nano, Mini and Pro models are state-of-the-art among open-source models (under the reported settings) in GUI grounding, desktop execution and web interaction. I am also responsible for the CUA and Web Agent capabilities of Nex-N2.5.

Before that, I was a Principal Researcher in the Data, Knowledge and Intelligence (DKI) group at Microsoft, where I was the lead developer and algorithm researcher of UFO, the first open-source Windows GUI agent / CUA framework, and drove its evolution through UFO² and UFO³ into a cross-platform, cross-device Desktop AgentOS with 10K+ GitHub stars. I also worked on AIOps: anomaly detection, failure diagnosis and root cause analysis for cloud services. I maintain a public survey on LLM-brained GUI agents ( Website) and wrote the first book that systematically introduces GUI agents.

Earlier, I was a research scientist at Lightspeed and Quantum Studio (光子工作室), Tencent, where I developed matchmaking systems for the game League of Legends: Wild Rift. I hold a PhD from the School of Informatics at the University of Edinburgh, supervised by Prof. Paul Patras and Rik Sarkar, an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the same institution, and a BEng in Telecommunication Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

For any inquiries, please feel free to reach me at vyokky at 163 dot com.

NorthCUA

Turbocharging Computer-Use Agents through Experience at Scale

An experience-driven computer-use agent system for desktop applications and the web: a unified harness that executes and verifies long-horizon tasks, an experience flywheel that turns weak spots into training data, and staged post-training that turns it into stronger policies. Its open models are state-of-the-art among open-source models (reported settings) in GUI grounding, desktop execution and web interaction.
